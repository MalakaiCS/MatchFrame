# Scorer guide

Open **/score** on a phone or tablet. Add it to the home screen for a full-screen app; after the first visit it
opens even without a connection.

## Your matches

The list has a search box (team, round or ground) and a competition picker at the top, then three tabs, each
with its count:

* **Now & next**: matches live now, every match today (finished and cancelled ones too) and everything still to
  come, nearest first.
* **Not played**: matches whose day has passed without anyone starting them, and postponed matches.
* **Results**: finished, abandoned and cancelled matches of the last month, newest first.

The matches are listed by day (*Today*, *Tomorrow*, then the date). Each is a ticker: on the left the kick-off time
or its state (*LIVE*, *FT*, *Running late* when kick-off has passed and nobody has started it, *Not started*,
*Cancelled*, *Postponed*); then both teams, each with its colour, crest, name and, once the match has started, its
score; and the competition, round and ground underneath. The ⋮ menu opens the scorer, the line-ups or, for
producers, the match page. A ticker also says when you only record **statistics** or **view** a match. Producers
have a **+ Match** button for a new match.

## Taking control

Only one device controls a match at a time. If someone else holds it you will see who; a producer can take over
(with a reason). If your control moves to another device, your unsent actions are **kept and not applied** —
you can review them. Statistics-only scorers never need control: their counts sit alongside the main scorer's
record and never change the score, clock or period.

## Line-ups

*Line-ups* in the bar at the bottom of the match screen opens the squad for each team. Tap **Start**, **Bench** or **Out** for each
player, mark the captain with **C**, and save. *Use last line-up* copies the team's previous match; *Use team
default* copies the saved default. Before the start the app reminds you if a line-up is incomplete, because goals,
cards and substitutions can only name players who are listed.

## The match screen

At the top: back to your matches, the match and its competition, and the connection (*Online*, *Offline*) with
*All changes saved* or the number of actions still to send. Under it the **scoreboard**: each team in its own
block with its colour down the outside edge, its crest, letters and score, and between them the period with the
clock (before kick-off, the kick-off time). Sin-bin countdowns, fouls and timeouts sit under the team they belong
to. During play the clock's controls come next: **−1′**, **Start / Pause / Resume**, **+1′** (each audited) and
**correct the clock** (✎). The bar fixed to the bottom of the screen has **Undo**, **Events** (the list of what has
been recorded), **Line-ups**, **Details** (the match, your preferences and the other options) and, during play, the
**End 1st Half** button (it names the period).

## Before the match and in breaks

The screen shows the match details (competition, teams, ground, date, time) and the run-down to the next period:
**Pre-match**, **Players entering** and **About to start**, numbered, each with **Skip** beside it, then
**Start 1st Half**.
Tap a step when it happens (the graphics for it play), or skip it; the half starts once every step is done or
skipped. A skipped step can be taken back with *Undo* until you start. At half time the same run-down has *Players
entering (2nd half)*, *About to start (2nd half)* and **Start 2nd Half**, so the line-ups and kick-off graphics
play for the second half too. *More options* holds the rest (Ready, Postponed, Abandoned — the last two ask
first). A red banner says when the starting line-ups are not filled in, with a link to the line-ups.

## Scoring

* **The event pad** has a tile per team for each event, the home team's on the left and the away team's on the
  right under their names, grouped into sections: for football **Score** (Goal, Own goal, Penalty scored, Penalty
  missed), **Possession**, **Attack** (Shot on target, Shot off target, Corner, Offside), **Defence** (Save, Foul),
  **Discipline** (Yellow card, Red card) and **Team** (Substitution). Each tile shows the event and that team's
  count, with the team's colour down its edge; the main scoring event is filled with the team's colour. Tap the
  tile of the team it happened to. Futsal adds 10 m kicks and timeouts; hockey has shots, penalty corners, circle
  entries and green, yellow and red cards; rugby league, AFL and netball have their own sections. Basketball keeps
  its court controls (+1/+2/+3, fouls by type, timeouts and the possession arrow) under the clock.
* **Tap it as it happens.** The highlights package is cut from the match video at the time each goal, card or
  chance was tapped: a goal's clip runs from 20 s before the tap to 8 s after (a red card's 12 s to 6 s, a
  chance's 10 s to 3 s), and its slow-motion replay shows from 11 s to 2 s before the tap, so it shows the goal
  when it was tapped 2 to 11 seconds after the ball went in. The
  producer can move a moment in 2 s steps if it was tapped early or late.
* **Statistics are one tap**: shots, saves, corners, fouls and offsides count at once, with no questions; add the
  player later from *Match events* (the person icon) if you want it on the record. The counts are the same numbers
  the half-time and full-time statistics graphics show.
* **Possession** is timed: tap the button of the team with the ball (*HAR ball*), and the other team's when it
  changes hands. The match clock between taps counts for the team that had the ball (stoppages and breaks never
  count); the team with the ball is marked, and a bar in both teams' colours shows the split live. A period change ends the
  spell, so tap again at the restart. Football, futsal, hockey and rugby league.
* For a score the number updates immediately; choose the player afterwards (*Later* is fine — players are never
  guessed). Players **on the field** are listed first, then the bench. Football goals then ask for the **assist**
  and the **type** (header, penalty, free kick…); both can be added later from *Match events* with *Details*.
  Cards and substitutions ask for the player first (*Skip player* if you are unsure); a second yellow for the same
  player is recorded as a second yellow, so the sending-off follows.
* **Added time** (football): when the fourth official holds up the board, tap *Added time* under the clock's controls and pick
  the minutes (+1 to +10, or type another). The board graphic goes on air for 8 s and the scorebug shows
  *ADDED +4* until the end of the half; the clock box shows the running added time *of +4*. Enter it again to
  change it.
* **Sin bin** (rugby league): tap *Sin bin* on the team's side; the countdown starts from the minutes in the rules
  (10 unless the competition says otherwise) and runs on playing time: it stops with the clock, time after the
  siren does not count, and what is left carries into the next half. It shows under the team on the scoreboard
  and on the scorebug. When the time is up the player is back where they were (on the field, or on the bench for a
  bench player), and an interchange can then take them off; until then they cannot come back through an
  interchange. A sin-binned player who is then sent off does not come back. Futsal's send-off shows its two
  minutes the same way.
* **Video review** (rugby league): tap *Video review* on the side of the team the decision is for, then *Try* or
  *Captain's challenge*. The review graphic goes on air (*TRY?*) and a yellow box above the event pad waits for
  the decision: **Try** puts the try on the score at once (pick who scored, as for any try) and the graphic turns
  green; **No try** turns it red with nothing on the score. A captain's challenge has *Successful* and
  *Unsuccessful*. The graphic leaves 6 s after the decision. One review at a time: give its decision before the
  next; one still open when the half ends is dropped.
* **Ending a period**: the *End …* button at the bottom opens a sheet with the next step first (Half Time, Full Time,
  Before Extra Time, Penalty Shootout) and the other ways on below it.
* **Level at the end**: when the score is level at the end of regulation and the rules have extra time or
  penalties (all finals, and knockout matches), the next step is **Before Extra Time** (or the shoot-out when there
  is no extra time), then **Penalty Shootout** after extra time; *Full Time* is below it. When a league match's
  rules let a draw stand, the screen asks *Does it need a winner?* and **Play extra time and penalties** switches
  this match to knockout on the spot. *More options* also has the *Knockout* switch (it cannot be turned off during
  extra time or the shoot-out). During the shoot-out each team has a card with **Scored** and **Missed**,
  the kicks so far as ticks and crosses with the next one ringed, and a line on where it stands (*Kick 3 of 5 · Harbour
  to kick*, *Harbour must score to stay in*, *Sudden death · round 6*). The team to kick next is outlined and marked *to kick*. As soon as
  one team can no longer be caught, the line says who won (*Northgate win 4–3 on penalties*), the buttons stop
  taking kicks and *End shoot-out* finishes the match; undo the deciding kick if it was recorded by mistake.
* Substitutions only offer players who can make the change: whoever is on the field comes off, whoever is on the
  bench comes on (rolling-substitution sports also list players who came off earlier).
* Basketball: +1/+2/+3 per team with the scorer named afterwards, the foul type (personal, offensive, technical,
  unsportsmanlike, disqualifying), timeouts with the number remaining, possession, rebounds and assists. The
  player list shows points and fouls, and marks anyone who has fouled out.
* Mistakes: straight after an action an **Undo** button floats for a few seconds and needs no reason; the bar's
  undo does the same while it is up. After that, the bar's undo (or undo on an event in *Match events*) asks
  for a reason. Corrections are audited and update graphics.
* Clock corrections need a reason; ±1 minute records its own. Basketball has a separate shot clock.

## Preferences

*Match details* → **Scoring preferences** opens your preferences, saved to your account and kept on the phone:

* **Vibrate** and **confirmation sounds** when an action is recorded (a different pattern for scores, cards and
  rejections).
* **Sunlight mode** — black on white with stronger colours for a phone in direct sun.
* **Low data mode** — turns off the live connection and refreshes the score every ten seconds instead; your
  actions still send immediately. The status pill says *Online · low data*. If a venue network blocks the live
  connection the app falls back to this by itself and says *Online · refreshing*.
* **Undo window** — how long the floating Undo button stays.

## Offline

The top of the screen shows **Offline** as soon as the connection drops. Keep scoring: actions are stored on the device
and marked pending; the score is shown as *provisional*. You may refresh the page while offline.
When the connection returns:

* if nobody changed the match meanwhile, your actions apply in order, once;
* if someone did (for example a producer took over), each offline action waits under **Needs review** —
  choose *Apply now* or *Discard*.

Late events never trigger an on-air celebration for something that happened long ago, but they do update the
record and can still produce correctly aligned highlight clips.

## Reminders

An hour before kick-off the scorers assigned to a match get an email with the link, and a notification on any
phone that has turned notifications on (Settings → Notifications, when the installation has push configured).
