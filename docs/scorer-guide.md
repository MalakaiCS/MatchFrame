# Scorer guide

Open **/score** on a phone or tablet and choose your match. Add it to the home screen for a full-screen app;
after the first visit it opens even without a connection. The list shows what you may do on each match:
score it, record **statistics only**, or **view only**.

## Taking control

Only one device controls a match at a time. If someone else holds it you will see who; a producer can take over
(with a reason). If your control moves to another device, your unsent actions are **kept and not applied** —
you can review them. Statistics-only scorers never need control: their counts sit alongside the main scorer's
record and never change the score, clock or period.

## Line-ups

*Line-ups* at the top of the match opens the squad for each team. Tap **Start**, **Bench** or **Out** for each
player, mark the captain with **C**, and save. *Use last line-up* copies the team's previous match; *Use team
default* copies the saved default. Before the start the app reminds you if a line-up is incomplete, because goals,
cards and substitutions can only name players who are listed.

## Scoring

* The big **Next:** button follows the match (Pre-match → Players entering → About to start → 1st Half → Half
  Time → …); *More options* holds the rest, including Abandoned. Starting a period starts the clock from the
  server's anchor, so refreshing or locking the phone does not change it. If a period runs more than ten minutes
  over, the app asks whether you meant to end it.
* **Two team columns**: the home team on the left, the away team on the right, each in its colour. A column has
  the team's scoring buttons (Goal; Try and Conversion; Goal and Behind), then one row per statistic with its
  count beside it. Football: Possession, Shots on target, Shots off target, Fouls, Yellow cards, Red cards,
  Offsides, Corners and Saves. Futsal and hockey have their own (no offsides; hockey has green cards, penalty
  corners and circle entries), as do rugby league, AFL and netball. *More* under a column holds the rarer events
  (penalty scored or missed, own goal, second yellow, substitution, timeout…). Basketball keeps its court layout
  (+1/+2/+3, fouls by type, timeouts and the possession arrow).
* **Statistics are one tap**: shots, fouls, offsides, corners and saves count at once, with no questions; add the
  player later from *Recent* (the person icon) if you want it on the record. The counts are the same numbers the
  half-time and full-time statistics graphics show.
* **Possession** is timed: tap *Possession* on the side of the team with the ball, and tap the other side when it
  changes hands. The match clock between taps counts for the team that had the ball (stoppages and breaks never
  count), the row says *Has the ball* and both rows show the share live. A period change ends the spell, so tap
  again at the restart. Undo a wrong tap like any other action. Football, futsal, hockey and rugby league.
* For a score the number updates immediately; choose the player afterwards (*Later* is fine — players are never
  guessed). Players **on the field** are listed first, then the bench. Football goals then ask for the **assist**
  and the **type** (header, penalty, free kick…); both can be added later from *Recent* with *Details*. Cards ask
  for the player first (*Skip player* if you are unsure). *Stoppage time* sits under the columns.
* **Level at the end**: when the score is level at the end of regulation and the rules have extra time or
  penalties (all finals, and knockout matches), *Next:* is **Before Extra Time** (or the shoot-out when there is no
  extra time), then **Penalty Shootout** after extra time; *Full Time* stays under *More options*. When a league
  match's rules let a draw stand, the phone asks *Does it need a winner?* and **Play extra time and penalties**
  switches this match to knockout on the spot. *More options* also has the *Knockout* switch (it cannot be
  turned off during extra time or the shoot-out). During the shoot-out each column has **Scored** and
  **Missed**, with the kicks shown as ticks and crosses.
* Substitutions only offer players who can make the change: whoever is on the field comes off, whoever is on the
  bench comes on (rolling-substitution sports also list players who came off earlier).
* Basketball: +1/+2/+3 per team with the scorer named afterwards, the foul type (personal, offensive, technical,
  unsportsmanlike, disqualifying), timeouts with the number remaining, possession, rebounds and assists. The
  player list shows points and fouls, and marks anyone who has fouled out.
* Between halves use *Players entering* and *About to start* again before *2nd Half*, so the lineups and the
  kick-off graphics play for the second half too.
* Mistakes: straight after an action an **Undo** button floats for a few seconds and needs no reason. Later, use
  undo on the event in *Recent* and give a reason. Corrections are audited and update graphics.
* Clock corrections need a reason; basketball has a separate shot clock.

## Preferences

The gear button opens your preferences, saved to your account and kept on the phone:

* **Vibrate** and **confirmation sounds** when an action is recorded (a different pattern for scores, cards and
  rejections).
* **Sunlight mode** — black on white with stronger colours for a phone in direct sun.
* **Low data mode** — turns off the live connection and refreshes the score every ten seconds instead; your
  actions still send immediately. The status pill says *Online · low data*. If a venue network blocks the live
  connection the app falls back to this by itself and says *Online · refreshing*.
* **Undo window** — how long the floating Undo button stays.

## Offline

The status bar shows **Offline** as soon as the connection drops. Keep scoring: actions are stored on the device
and marked pending; the score is shown as *provisional*. You may refresh the page while offline.
When the connection returns:

* if nobody changed the match meanwhile, your actions apply in order, once;
* if someone did (for example a producer took over), each offline action waits under **Needs review** —
  choose *Apply now* or *Discard*.

Late events never trigger an on-air celebration for something that happened long ago, but they do update the
record and can still produce correctly aligned highlight clips.

## Reminders

An hour before kick-off the scorers assigned to a match get an email with the link, and a notification on any
phone that has turned notifications on (Settings → Notifications, when the installation has push configured).
